# ☕ Coffee Shop Sales Analysis – Excel Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

**Coffee Shop Sales Analysis** is an interactive **Microsoft Excel Data Analytics project** developed to analyze coffee shop sales, customer footfall, product performance, store performance, order patterns, and customer purchasing behavior.

The dashboard transforms raw transaction data into meaningful business insights using **Excel Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, Slicers, KPI cards, and data visualization techniques**.

This project is suitable as a portfolio project for demonstrating practical **Data Analyst / Business Analyst skills**.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

A coffee shop needs to understand its sales and customer behavior in order to improve revenue, product strategy, staffing, and store performance.

This project answers key business questions such as:

- What is the total sales generated?
- How many customers visited the coffee shop?
- What is the average bill per person?
- How many products does a customer purchase on average?
- Which hours have the highest order quantity?
- Which product categories contribute the most to sales?
- Which product sizes are ordered most frequently?
- Which store locations generate the highest sales and footfall?
- Which products generate the highest sales?
- Which weekdays receive the highest number of orders?

## 📈 Dashboard Analysis

### 1. Quantity Ordered Based on Hours

The hourly trend analyzes the quantity of products ordered throughout the day.

The dashboard shows order activity from **6 AM to 8 PM**.

This analysis helps identify:

- Peak ordering hours
- Low-demand periods
- Suitable staffing hours
- Opportunities for time-based promotions

The highest order quantity occurs around the **morning hours**, particularly around **9–10 AM**.

## 🔍 Key Insights

Based on the dashboard:

- Total sales are approximately **$698.81K**.
- Total customer footfall is **149,116**.
- The average bill per person is **$4.69**.
- Customers purchase approximately **1.44 products per person** on average.
- Morning hours show strong order activity, with peak demand around **9–10 AM**.
- **Coffee** is the largest contributor to category sales.
- **Regular and Large** sizes account for the largest portions of orders.
- **Hell's Kitchen** has the highest sales among the displayed store locations.
- **Barista Espresso** is the highest-selling product among the top products shown.
- **Friday** has the highest number of orders, while Saturday has the lowest.
- Store-level analysis shows that sales and footfall can be compared to evaluate operational performance.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the coffee shop can:

1. **Increase staffing during peak morning hours** to handle higher order volumes.
2. **Promote high-performing products** such as Barista Espresso and other top-selling beverages.
3. **Focus inventory planning on high-performing categories**, especially Coffee.
4. **Use weekday-specific promotions** to improve sales on lower-order days.
5. **Optimize product-size inventory** based on customer ordering preferences.
6. **Compare store performance regularly** to identify opportunities for improving lower-performing locations.
7. **Use peak-hour promotions** to increase revenue during high-demand periods.
8. **Analyze customer footfall together with sales** to understand revenue performance at each location.
